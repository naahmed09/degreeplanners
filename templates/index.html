Our Mission

Get every SAU student to graduation by the shortest, clearest path — with no wasted semesters, no wasted tuition, and no guesswork.

Save time

Courses that unlock the most other courses are placed first, so a late prerequisite never quietly adds a semester.

Save money

Plans are built from your degree's course list, and retakes and gaps are flagged up front, before they turn into extra tuition.

Cut the confusion

One semester-by-semester plan, a prerequisite map, and a plain-language reason for anything that can't be placed.

A planning aid, not an official record. Review every plan with your advisor and the current catalog before registering.

Before you start

This planner helps you draft a schedule. Its results are estimates, not an official degree audit. Check with your advisor and the official catalog before you register.

  • No account, no cookies, no tracking and no ads.
  • What you type is sent to this server only to build your plan, and is not stored there.
  • Saving your work on this device is off unless you turn it on.
  • It is open source: anyone can read the code and check these claims.
